    The communities

    MAQC Society

    The society behind MAQC 2026 — “Reproducibility is science. Science demands reproducibility.” · themaqc.org

    • MAQC ML Working Group — the society’s machine learning working group, and the natural home for follow-up on this session

    ELIXIR

    The European life-sciences research infrastructure · elixir-europe.org

    • AI Ecosystem Focus Group — the current home for this work, successor to the ML Focus Group since October 2025
    • Machine Learning Focus Group — developed DOME and OSAI; ran 2019–2025

    BioComputingUP, University of Padua

    The group maintaining the DOME Registry and dome-schema · protein.bio.unipd.it
