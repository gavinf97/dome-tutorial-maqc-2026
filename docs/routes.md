# Hands-on: Submission Routes

**Pick one route, annotate a paper, and review what you produce.**

---

There is no single right way to produce a DOME transparency report. In the
35-minute hands-on exercise, choose **one** of the three routes below — ideally
with a paper of your own. Whichever route you pick, a human reviews every field
before anything is deposited.

| | **Route A — Manual** | **Route B — DOME Copilot** | **Route C — Agent Skill** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What drives it | You, reading the paper | A mid-sized LLM (Mistral Small 3.1 24B) | A frontier agent (Claude or similar) |
| Setup needed | An [LS Login](https://lifescience-ri.eu/ls-login/) or DOME Wizard account | Web login, nothing installed | Python 3.10+, an agent platform |
| Time per paper | 30–90 min | ~2 min to draft, then human review | ~5–15 min, then human review |
| Cost | Free | Cheap, self-hostable, scales to a corpus | Higher per paper; needs model credits |
| Covers OSAI too | No | No | Yes — DOME plus OSAI suggestions |
| Human review | *Is* the work | **Required** | **Required** |
| Step by step | [Route A](chapters/chapter_06_route_manual.md) | [Route B](chapters/chapter_07_route_copilot.md) | [Route C](chapters/chapter_08_route_agent.md) |

The tool links and the DOME Copilot shared login are on the
[Session Assets](assets.md) page.
